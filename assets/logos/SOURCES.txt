Affiliation emblems (retrieved 14 November 2026)

These logos identify the institutions named in Ali SaraerToosi's research
experience and education. Trademarks belong to their respective organizations.
Colors and aspect ratios are preserved. ICO files were converted to PNG without
resizing; the EHT file displays the emblem portion of the published vector.

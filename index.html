01 / Profile

About me

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto and the Vector Institute, advised by Aviad Levis and David Lindell. I’m a member of Physics-Informed Vision and Imaging (PI-Vision) and the Toronto Computational Imaging Group (TCIG). Before my PhD, I studied physics at Sharif University of Technology and completed an MSc at the University of Waterloo and Perimeter Institute.

My background and research span physics, artificial intelligence, and computer vision. At Autodesk Research, I work on topology optimization for 3D generative modeling and agentic procedural 3D design. My academic projects include neural representations of physical dynamics, black-hole imaging, and physical-parameter inference.
